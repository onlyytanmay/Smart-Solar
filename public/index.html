How it works

From unused rooftop to shared community power

Four steps connect individual solar systems into one managed network.

Connect

Register your flat and solar system details in minutes.

Monitor

See generation, consumption, battery level and surplus on one dashboard.

Share

Offer surplus to neighbours and accept or reject requests.

Maintain

Track inspections, reminders and reported issues for every panel.

Platform features

Everything a rooftop solar owner needs

⚡

Live energy monitoring

Generation, consumption and battery charging in one view with daily, weekly and monthly charts.

🔋

Battery & reserve logic

A clear energy-balance model keeps a reserve and never counts stored energy twice.

🤝

Community sharing

Browse neighbours' surplus, send requests, and track shared energy history.

🛠

Maintenance tracking

Panel health, inspection dates, reminders and a simple issue-reporting workflow.

🔔

Smart notifications

Admin oversight

Residents, buildings, installations, shared energy and maintenance at a glance.
